## 2. In-Depth Evaluation of the 6 Options

### Option 4: Better Borrower Targeting (The Winning Allocation)
* **The Opportunity**: Build dynamic multi-stage propensity models, real-time optimal contact timing algorithms, and bureau-integrated payment capability scoring.
* **Capital Breakdown**: ₹3.5 Cr Capex (Feature store, MLOps infrastructure, data engineering) + ₹6.5 Cr Opex (Bureau data pulls, external debt registry APIs, cloud compute).
* **Financial Return**: Generates **+1.6% recovery rate lift** across ₹250 Cr addressable exposure, yielding **₹4.00 Cr in gross incremental cash** and **+₹2.75 Cr Net Benefit** (**27.5% ROI**). Break-even achieved in **8.5 months**.
* **Risk Profile**: Low regulatory risk (no consumer harassment); primary operational risk is model drift and score latency, mitigated by continuous re-training pipelines.

---

### Option 5: WhatsApp & Digital Engagement (Runner-Up)
* **The Opportunity**: Automated conversational WhatsApp payment flows, interactive payment arrangement negotiation, and one-click UPI links.
* **Financial Return**: **₹2.52 Cr gross cash** (+1.4% lift on ₹180 Cr exposure), Net Benefit: **+₹1.52 Cr** (**15.2% ROI**; break-even in 9.8 months).
* **Limitations**: Meta API conversational pricing (₹0.80/conversation) eats into operating margin; risk of template blocking if consumers report spam.

---

### Option 2: More Collection Agents (Why Headcount Expansion Fails)
* **The Trap**: Operations instinctively requests hiring 150 additional collection agents.
* **The Reality**:
  1. **Trainee Drag**: Trainees take 4.5 months to achieve baseline proficiency, generating 60% lower recovery per hour than seniors.
  2. **Attrition**: Annual agent churn is 38%, forcing continuous onboarding overhead.
  3. **Economics**: ₹8.5 Cr annual salaries + ₹1.5 Cr workstation seats yields only ₹1.20 Cr incremental recovery, resulting in an expected **loss of ₹0.80 Cr (-8% ROI)**.
  4. **Compliance**: Multiplies verbal harassment complaints and calling hours infractions under RBI scrutiny.

---

### Option 6: Field Operations (Why Physical Door-to-Door Fails at Scale)
* **The Trap**: Field visits achieve the highest raw PTP kept rate (62%).
* **The Reality**: Extreme unit economics (₹1,800 fully loaded cost per visit). Net cash yield is negative on any delinquent ticket below ₹50,000. Additionally, physical home visits carry critical legal liability under RBI Fair Practices Code for microfinance and digital lending.

## 3. Staged Implementation & De-Risking Protocol

Rather than releasing the full ₹10.0 Crore capital expenditure unconditionally, leadership should execute a **Staged Gate-Review Strategy**:

```
[Phase 1: 90-Day Randomized Controlled Pilot]
  ├── Capital Drawdown: ₹2.00 Crore (Tranche 1)
  ├── Population: 50,000 randomly selected delinquent accounts
  ├── Methodology: Strict A/B test with 10% un-targeted holdout control
  └── Decision Gate: Must achieve statistically verified lift >= +1.0 pp (p < 0.05)
            │
            ├── IF SUCCESSFUL: Release Remaining ₹8.00 Crore for Full Scale Rollout
            └── IF UNSUCCESSFUL: Pivot Capital to Option 5 (WhatsApp Conversational Links)
```
