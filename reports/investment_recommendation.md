## 1. Executive Summary & Financial Audit

Leadership has budgeted ₹10 Crore to invest in strategic collections enhancements. A rigorous financial audit reveals a critical insight: **sinking the full ₹10 Crore into any single initiative in Year 1 fails to break even under conservative unit economics.** 

### Standardized Financial Formulas
$$\text{Incremental Net Benefit} = \text{Gross Incremental Recovery} - \text{Total Incremental Investment Cost}$$
$$\text{ROI (\%)} = \frac{\text{Incremental Net Benefit}}{\text{Total Incremental Investment Cost}} \times 100\%$$
$$\text{Break-even (Months)} = \text{Ramp-up Months} + \frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Monthly Gross Run-rate}}$$

### Mathematical Reconciliation of Legacy Claims
Legacy documentation conflated three incompatible assumptions:
1. **The Baseline Outlay Contradiction**: If Gross Recovery is **₹4.00 Cr** (1.6% lift on ₹250 Cr exposure) and Total Cost is **₹10.00 Cr**, the true 1-year Net Benefit is:
   $$\text{Net Benefit} = ₹4.00\text{ Cr} - ₹10.00\text{ Cr} = \mathbf{-₹6.00\text{ Cr}} \quad (\mathbf{-60.0\%\text{ ROI}})$$
2. **The 27.5% ROI Condition**: For a ₹10.0 Cr capital expenditure to yield a **+₹2.75 Cr Net Benefit** (**+27.5% ROI**), Gross Incremental Recovery must reach:
   $$\text{Required Gross Cash} = \text{Cost} + \text{Net Benefit} = ₹10.00\text{ Cr} + ₹2.75\text{ Cr} = \mathbf{₹12.75\text{ Cr}}$$
   Across ₹250 Cr addressable exposure, this requires an incremental recovery lift of **5.10%** (510 bps).
3. **The Prudent Leadership Solution**: To capture the high upside of Option 4 without risking a ₹6.0 Cr Year-1 capital loss, leadership must deploy an immediate **₹2.0 Crore Staged Randomized Pilot** (50,000 accounts, ₹60 Cr exposure) that breaks even within **5.7 months** before committing the remaining ₹8.0 Crore.

## 3. In-Depth Operational & Risk Evaluation

### Option 4: Better Borrower Targeting (Why It Wins)
* **Operational Mechanism**: Machine-learning propensity-to-pay models, multi-bureau bureau pull prioritization, and dynamic time-of-day contactability routing.
* **Capital Breakdown (Full Scale)**: ₹3.5 Cr Capex (Feature store, MLOps orchestration, data pipeline engineering) + ₹6.5 Cr Opex (Bureau scoring API pulls, alternative data feeds, cloud compute).
* **Compliance Exposure**: **Low**. Algorithmic scoring eliminates aggressive dialing tactics, call frequency spamming, and verbal harassment risks under RBI Fair Practices guidelines.
* **Why Others Fall Short**: Adding agents (Option 2) increases operating friction due to 38% annual churn and 40% lower trainee productivity. Field operations (Option 6) carries extreme unit costs (₹1,800/visit) and severe legal exposure under RBI lending regulations.

## 4. The Recommended Solution: ₹2.0 Crore Staged Pilot

To eliminate unhedged capital risk, we recommend structuring the commitment as a **Two-Stage Gate-Review Deployment**:

```
[STAGE 1: ₹2.0 Cr Randomized Controlled Pilot (Months 1–6)]
  ├── Capital Outlay: ₹2.00 Crore (₹0.75 Cr Setup Capex + ₹1.25 Cr 6-Mo Opex)
  ├── Eligible Cohort: 50,000 Delinquent Accounts in DPD 31–90
  ├── Addressable Exposure: ₹60.0 Crore
  ├── Expected Lift Rate: +3.5% on targeted sub-cohort
  ├── Gross Recovery: ₹2.10 Crore
  ├── Net Benefit: +₹0.10 Crore (+5.0% ROI in Month 6)
  └── Break-even: 5.7 Months
            │
            ├── DECISION GATE: Did randomized holdout control (10%) confirm causal lift >= +2.5% (p < 0.05)?
            │
            ├── YES (PASS) ──────► Release Remaining ₹8.00 Crore (Stage 2 Expansion)
            │                      - Projected Full-Scale Gross Recovery: ₹11.40 Cr
            │                      - Projected Net Benefit: +₹3.40 Cr (34.0% ROI)
            │                      - Full-Cycle Break-even: 8.5 Months
            │
            └── NO (FAIL)  ──────► Cap Losses at ₹2.0 Cr; Pivot Remaining ₹8.0 Cr to WhatsApp Conversational Payment Links
```

### Staged Pilot Unit Economics
* **Monthly Gross Cash Generation**: ₹0.42 Cr/month across active dialing months (Months 2–6).
* **Payback Period**: 5.7 months from initial pilot launch.
* **Holdout Design**: 10% strictly uncontacted randomized holdout control group ensures that seasonal repayments and macro factors are not misattributed to algorithmic lift.
